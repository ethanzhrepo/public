Sayloom Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

Sayloom is a local-first macOS transcription, translation, and AI assistant app. The developer does not operate an analytics, advertising, account, or content-processing server for Sayloom and does not sell personal information.

Audio and transcripts

Sayloom accesses microphone or system audio only after the user starts capture and grants the relevant macOS permission. When a local model is selected, audio, transcripts, translations, and AI prompts are processed on the Mac.

If the user enables an OpenAI-compatible cloud provider, the content needed for that feature may be sent directly from the Mac to the endpoint configured by the user. Sayloom does not proxy or receive those requests. Processing by that provider is governed by the provider's own privacy policy and the user's agreement with it.

Information stored on the Mac

  • App preferences, session metadata, and transcripts are stored in the app's local sandbox.
  • API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain when the user chooses to save them.
  • Downloaded speech and language models are stored locally and can be removed by the user.
  • Sayloom does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Model downloads and purchases

Model downloads may connect to the model host identified in the app. The host may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address and request metadata, but Sayloom does not include captured audio or transcript content in model-download requests.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Sayloom receives purchase and entitlement status from StoreKit but does not receive payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains on the Mac until the user deletes it in Sayloom or removes the app's data. Saved provider credentials can be removed from the app's settings or macOS Keychain. Data sent to a user-configured provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion controls.

Children

Sayloom is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Sayloom.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when Sayloom's privacy practices materially change. For privacy or support questions, please use the Sayloom support page and avoid posting confidential information in a public issue.

Sayloom 隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日

概述

Sayloom 是一款本地优先的 macOS 转录、翻译和 AI 助手应用。开发者不为 Sayloom 运营分析、广告、账号或内容处理服务器，也不会出售个人信息。

音频与转录内容

Sayloom 只会在用户主动开始捕获并授予相应 macOS 权限后访问麦克风或系统音频。选择本地模型时，音频、转录、翻译及 AI 提示均在这台 Mac 上处理。

如果用户启用兼容 OpenAI 的云端服务，相关功能所需内容可能会从 Mac 直接发送到用户配置的服务地址。Sayloom 不会代理或接收这些请求。服务商如何处理数据，受其隐私政策以及用户与该服务商之间的协议约束。

存储在 Mac 上的信息

  • 应用偏好、会话元数据和转录内容存储在应用的本地沙盒中。
  • 用户选择保存 API Key 时，密钥会存入 macOS 钥匙串。
  • 下载的语音与语言模型保存在本地，用户可以将其删除。
  • Sayloom 不包含第三方广告或跨应用跟踪。

模型下载与购买

模型下载可能会连接应用中标明的模型托管服务。托管方可能收到 IP 地址、请求元数据等常规网络信息，但 Sayloom 不会在模型下载请求中包含捕获的音频或转录内容。

购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。Sayloom 会通过 StoreKit 获取购买和权益状态，但不会收到银行卡资料。

保留与删除

本地内容会保留在 Mac 上，直到用户在 Sayloom 中删除或移除应用数据。已保存的服务商凭据可以在应用设置或 macOS 钥匙串中移除。发送给用户所配置服务商的数据，受该服务商的保留与删除机制约束。

儿童隐私

Sayloom 是通用效率工具，并非面向儿童。开发者不会有意通过 Sayloom 收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新与联系

如果 Sayloom 的隐私实践发生重大变化，本页面会相应更新。如有隐私或支持问题，请使用 Sayloom 支持页面，并避免在公开 Issue 中发布机密信息。
